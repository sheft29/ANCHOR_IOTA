What we collect

  • What you tell us at the start: the first name you give, and your answers to the onboarding questions (what you'd like more of, what leaves you feeling like yourself, your time, your obstacles, and an optional "one small thing").
  • Your account, if you choose to create one: your email address (for a passwordless sign-in link) or, if you use Google sign-in, the basic profile Google returns. You can use Anchor without an account; your data then stays on your device.
  • Your practice: the daily action you were shown, whether you completed it, your rating of whether it was worth doing, your alignment response, and any "what was off" note. This is what lets Anchor choose and improve your next action.
  • What you write: your journal lines, your values, and your goal. These are free text and may be personal.

We do not collect your contacts, location, advertising identifiers, or health-app data, and Anchor does not use clinical screening instruments.

How we use it

  • To choose one small action for you each day and to adapt future suggestions to your feedback.
  • To let you see your own history and what Anchor has learned about your preferences.
  • To understand, in aggregate and without identifying you, whether Anchor is helpful, so we can improve it. This is a feasibility and acceptability evaluation, not a research study, and Anchor makes no efficacy claims.

How the recommendation works, and AI

Anchor's suggestions come from a transparent rules-and-weights model: your answers set initial preferences, and your feedback adjusts weights for different kinds of activity over time. It is not generative AI, and it does not profile you beyond the preferences described here. If we later add any AI feature (for example, translation for other languages), we will say so here and in the app.

Where your data lives, and security

  • On your device by default (browser storage). If you never sign in, it does not leave your device.
  • If you sign in, your data also syncs to our database provider, Supabase (PostgreSQL). Access is restricted by row-level security so that only your signed-in account can read or write your data. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest.
  • Optional passphrase encryption: you can turn on a passphrase that encrypts your journal, values, and goal on your device before they sync, so no one but you, not even us, can read them. If you forget that passphrase, that writing cannot be recovered.

Sharing

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers. We use service providers (such as Supabase for hosting and database, and an email provider for sign-in links) that process data only to run Anchor. We will not share your individual data otherwise, except if required by law.

Your choices

  • See and edit everything stored about you in the You tab ("Memory").
  • Delete your data from your device at any time ("Delete all my data"). If you have an account, request account and server-side deletion at [contact email] [add one-tap in-app account deletion before launch].
  • Use Anchor without an account, keeping everything on your device.
